Why, oh why does Google try to figure out what language I speak based on my IP rather than using my browser\'s language setting or my google account language? Any time I delete cookies and then go to blogger, I\'m greeted by this:\
\

<a href="https://3.bp.blogspot.com/-7e4bHccEQPI/Tkyzcy5FzgI/AAAAAAAAAnY/ZscezNgz4cc/s1600/Screen%2Bshot%2B2011-08-18%2Bat%2B9.35.59%2BAM.png" data-imageanchor="1" style="margin-left:1em; margin-right:1em"><img src="https://3.bp.blogspot.com/-7e4bHccEQPI/Tkyzcy5FzgI/AAAAAAAAAnY/ZscezNgz4cc/s400/Screen%2Bshot%2B2011-08-18%2Bat%2B9.35.59%2BAM.png" data-border="0" width="400" height="52" /></a>

\
The same goes for searching the web with Google \-- it\'s always trying to send me to google.com.sa and give me search results in Arabic. Even if I type \'www.google.com\' into the browser bar; it redirects me!\
This even though I\'m logged into my Google account, which specifies English as my native language:\

<a href="https://3.bp.blogspot.com/-yX_tTvamdEs/Tky0ibuLFyI/AAAAAAAAAng/BhsONaGi_C8/s1600/Screen%2Bshot%2B2011-08-18%2Bat%2B9.42.15%2BAM.png" data-imageanchor="1" style="margin-left:1em; margin-right:1em"><img src="https://3.bp.blogspot.com/-yX_tTvamdEs/Tky0ibuLFyI/AAAAAAAAAng/BhsONaGi_C8/s400/Screen%2Bshot%2B2011-08-18%2Bat%2B9.42.15%2BAM.png" data-border="0" width="400" height="142" /></a>

\
It\'s maddening. And there are plenty of pages like [this one](https://www.google.com/support/forum/p/blogger/thread?tid=3649c0e03b8f27bc&hl=en) on Google\'s own websites where people have pointed out the problem, but to no avail.\
\
A tip for others who have this problem: I go to encrypted.google.com to search. That one\'s always in English (which also doesn\'t really make sense). I haven\'t found a good solution for Blogger, except to try all the available links until I get the right one. If this blog ever disappears, it\'s probably because I clicked \'flag this blog as abusive\' by accident too many times.
